# Credits

## Inspiration

- **[caelestia](https://github.com/caelestia-dots/caelestia)**: the desktop
  started on caelestia's dots. It has moved on since, and kept some of its look
  and defaults.
- **[HyDE](https://github.com/HyDE-Project/HyDE)**: much of the look, the
  keybinds and the animations.
- **[mpv-image-config](https://github.com/guidocella/mpv-image-config)**: mpv
  as an image viewer.

## Included from others

- The `noctalia-card` login theme ([`linux/sddm/themes/noctalia-card`](linux/sddm/themes/noctalia-card/README.md)) is a fork
  of the "locklike" theme from
  [caelestia-sddm](https://github.com/ItsABigIgloo/caelestia-sddm) by AkhiLeith
  (GPL-3.0).
- The Arch Updater bar plugin ([`linux/noctalia/plugins/arch-updater`](linux/noctalia/plugins/arch-updater/README.md)) is a copy
  of yuuto's plugin for Noctalia (MIT).
- The animation presets in [`linux/hypr/animations`](linux/hypr/animations) are HyDE's (GPL-3.0), and
  [`linux/hypr/window-mute`](linux/hypr/window-mute) is a port of HyDE's `window.mute.py`.
- The Spotify theme's CSS ([`linux/noctalia/spicetify-user.css`](linux/noctalia/spicetify-user.css))
  comes from caelestia's Spotify theme (GPL-3.0).
- Google Sans Flex by Google (OFL-1.1), in [`linux/noctalia/fonts`](linux/noctalia/fonts) and the login
  theme.
