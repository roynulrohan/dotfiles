# Credits

My own files are under the MIT licence ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). The work below
comes from others and keeps its own licence.

## Inspiration

- **[caelestia](https://github.com/caelestia-dots/caelestia)**: the desktop
  started on caelestia's dots. It has moved on since, and kept some of its look
  and its lock screen design.
- **[HyDE](https://github.com/HyDE-Project/HyDE)**: much of the look, the
  keybinds and the animations.
- **[mpv-image-config](https://github.com/guidocella/mpv-image-config)**: mpv
  as an image viewer.

## Included from others

- The Hyprland package ([`linux/hypr`](linux/hypr/SETUP.md)) is GPL-3.0. Its
  modules, `hyprland.lua`, `variables.lua`, `utils/`, `scheme/` and the
  `caelestia` animation preset come from
  [caelestia](https://github.com/caelestia-dots/caelestia)'s Hyprland config.
  The other animation presets in [`linux/hypr/animations`](linux/hypr/animations)
  are HyDE's, and [`linux/hypr/window-mute`](linux/hypr/window-mute) is a port of
  HyDE's `window.mute.py`.
- The GTK and Spotify colour templates
  ([`gtk.css`](linux/noctalia/templates/gtk.css),
  [`spicetify.ini`](linux/noctalia/templates/spicetify.ini)) and the Spotify
  theme's CSS ([`linux/noctalia/spicetify-user.css`](linux/noctalia/spicetify-user.css))
  come from caelestia (GPL-3.0).
- The btop and Qt colour templates
  ([`btop.theme`](linux/noctalia/templates/btop.theme),
  [`qtengine.colors`](linux/noctalia/templates/qtengine.colors)) are copies of
  [Noctalia](https://github.com/noctalia-dev/noctalia-shell)'s built-in
  templates (MIT).
- The `noctalia-card` login theme ([`linux/sddm/themes/noctalia-card`](linux/sddm/themes/noctalia-card/README.md)) is a fork
  of the "locklike" theme from
  [caelestia-sddm](https://github.com/ItsABigIgloo/caelestia-sddm) by AkhiLeith
  (GPL-3.0).
- The Arch Updater bar plugin ([`linux/noctalia/plugins/arch-updater`](linux/noctalia/plugins/arch-updater/README.md)) is a copy
  of yuuto's plugin for Noctalia (MIT).
- The Discord theme template ([`linux/noctalia/templates/equibop.theme.css`](linux/noctalia/templates/equibop.theme.css))
  is a fork of refact0r's [Midnight](https://github.com/refact0r/midnight-discord) (MIT).
- The SketchyBar config ([`mac/wm/sketchybar`](mac/wm/sketchybar)) is adapted
  from [Felix Kratz's dotfiles](https://github.com/FelixKratz/dotfiles)
  (GPL-3.0). Its app icon map (`plugins/icon_map.sh`) comes from
  [sketchybar-app-font](https://github.com/kvndrsslr/sketchybar-app-font)
  (CC0-1.0).
- The Neovim config ([`shared/nvim`](shared/nvim/SETUP.md)) starts from the
  [LazyVim starter](https://github.com/LazyVim/starter) (Apache-2.0).
- Google Sans Flex by Google (OFL-1.1), in [`linux/noctalia/fonts`](linux/noctalia/fonts) and the login
  theme.
